Welcome

Hello! My name is Tyra Clary, and this is my GIS portfolio for GIST 604B – Open Source GIS at the University of Arizona.

This portfolio highlights my growth in GIS through projects involving QGIS, PostGIS, Python, GeoPandas, OpenStreetMap, Leaflet, and web mapping. Each project helped me build stronger technical skills while learning how to analyze, organize, and present spatial data in meaningful ways.

My background as a former Division 1 softball athlete has shaped the way I approach GIS work: with discipline, teamwork, communication, and a problem-solving mindset.

About Me

I am a GIS graduate student at the University of Arizona with a background in public health, athletics, and geospatial technology. I am interested in how GIS can be used to support public-sector work, improve decision-making, and make complex data easier to understand.

Through this course, I gained hands-on experience with open-source GIS tools, spatial databases, Python-based analysis, and interactive web mapping. I enjoy learning new tools and building projects that turn spatial data into something useful and visual.

Outside of GIS, my experience as a college softball athlete has taught me leadership, accountability, communication, and how to work hard toward long-term goals.
